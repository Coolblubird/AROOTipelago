# CATS: 
### An Unofficial Campaign of Feline Courage and Bravery
By Coolblubird 
and with the assistance of both the Leder Games Discord and the Archipelago Randomizer Discord. Art will be credited as placed.

## Overview:
The purpose of this project is to take what was first given by Mechanical Marquise v1.0 and take it to a different level. To limit the scope of this project, to make sure the project is feasible, we have to start from some place simple and work our way outwards. To the joy of our feline compatriots and to the sorrow of the rest of our factions in the woodland, we will only be writing this document from the perspective of the Marquise De Cat faction. If you dare, you may make modifications to make other factions work on this, and I encourage those efforts, but the project, for now, will focus on just the cats.

## Story:
“Welcome, Commander. The woodland front has been fruitful, but we feel a strong influence awakening in the woodland.” Your Catdoux assistant Mola spoke. His feline hands were carrying a map that was much larger than he was. “There have been whispers of the factions even using… machines, sir, to fight the war.”
By the time you had gotten out of your bed to process what was going on, you realized that the comfy life at the Marquisate’s capital may not be as well-respected here in the woodland. Mola places the map on the table in the center of your room, which you thought was intended for breakfast. 
“There, now look at these-” He reaches into his pocket and starts placing small tokens on the map to represent the various factions and approximate strength they have over the clearings; the Marquisate clearly had the overwhelming lead. “We need to press the advantage, Commander, now that you are here. What are your orders?” 
As you inspect the table, still in your purrjamas, you aren’t sure where to start, but maybe you’ll figure it out on your way or die trying.

## Campaign Setup:
-Decide the set of expansions you are going to play. The base game is considered an expansion for simplicity.
-Decide the set of Maps you are going to play on. You may choose this via “boards” (so adding both Fall and Winter because they share the same board) or just purely picking by map. 
    -You may use the Custom Map rules as written on page 225 in the ROOT:RPG [C] to create fast custom woodlands to battle in, just be wary of balance, and ignore rules for faction setup or anything not needed in ROOT, such as clearing names. For building spaces, use the following, but do know that it is mostly up to interpretation on how you want to implement the map:
        -For clearings with only one path, give them 1 building slot.
        -For clearings with few paths, give them 2 building slots.
        -For clearings with many paths, give them 2 building slots and 1 ruin slot.
    -You may also use any custom map you can find on the Leder Games Discord or elsewhere. Again, be wary of balance, but enjoy.
-You may exclude any number of factions you might consider unfair or easy, or you may play against them anyways to have the chance to grind for more Value to spend in the Workshop. For each faction skipped, you may give yourself a starting Value of 1. Skipped factions do not count towards Tiering in the Workshop. Suggested factions to exclude are:
    -Woodland Alliance
    -Riverfolk Company [C]
    -Lizard Cult [C]
    -Second Vagabond [C] (alternatively, if you are feeling brave, you may face both Vagabonds at once, but may Patrick Leder have mercy on your soul)
    -Corvid Conspiracy [C]
    -Twilight Council [C]
    -(Mechanical Marquise v1.0 [C] is excluded by default, unless…)



## Rounds of Play:
For each “Round” of play, until you have defeated each faction, do the following:
-Randomly decide on one faction to face that you have not faced yet and not excluded from the Campaign Setup
-Randomly decide on a map that the round will take place on from the options set aside in the campaign setup (alternatively, you may fight through each map in any order you want, but this is going with randomization in mind).
-Randomly determine which deck [C] you will be using for the round (or some combination of decks). You must remove the Dominance cards from the deck.
-Randomly determine one landmark [C] that hasn’t been used yet to be placed on the map in a random clearing. If all have been used, you may use any of them once again.
-Randomly determine how many Hirelings [C] and which ones will be used (as a suggestion, you may roll one of the battle dice to determine how many Hirelings will be played with, 0-3).
-As a reminder, standard Hireling rules apply, so don’t use the Forest Patrol, but let’s just say they may have some use elsewhere… (like in the Workshop)
-Set up ROOT as given through the standard Setup rules on the back of each faction board.
-For maps with fewer than 4 Ruin slots, remove the excess Ruins tokens randomly from the pool and set up as usual.
-For maps with more than 4 Ruin slots, you may add extra items either through an expansion’s extra Vagabond pieces (such as Riverfolk [C] or the Vagabond Upgrade Pack [C]) or you may play with them empty.
-Upon victory, pass to the next Round, stopping at the Workshop for upgrades before it begins. Upon defeat, lose one of your lives and retry the Round.

## The Workshop:
As the Marquise overtakes the woodland, there may be turns on Rounds where they are stuck with 8 wood during Birdsong. On turns within these rounds, any Wood tokens that cannot be placed due to the maximum number being already placed on the board are converted into “Value,” the bartered currency of the Woodland used to trade and create new technologies to help the Marquise stand up to greater and greater threats. By default, the Marquise will get 2 Value per defeat of any faction. The upgrades are tiered, so you must meet the condition to be able to buy from that tier. (if you are playing with less than 5 factions, simply skip the amount of Tiers to make up the difference, so playing with the base-game three factions has you start at Tier 3 and below, and go up one for each faction defeated.)

There are three types of upgrades: Crafted Cards, Hand Cards, Headstarts, and Other. 

Crafted Cards have you permanently start a Round with a specific stone-backed card in play, and they are worth a varying cost in Value. You may buy them in any order and may even skip any you don’t like. Normal restrictions and conditions apply (for example, you can only have one of the “Friend of the X” cards, and you may only have one of each crafted card). Brazen Demagogue (SD) and Codebreakers are removed from this list due to not being functional/worthwhile in two-player against bots. Royal Claim was removed because it breaks the game. (Optional Cards are also marked which set they come from, those with an EP are from “Exiles and Partisans” deck, while those with a SD are from the “Squires and Disciples” deck. All cards are sorted alphabetically after source deck.)

Headstarts are obtained in a specific order, given by the tier (i.e., you may only buy the upgrade if you have every previous upgrade before it), and cost the value of their upgrade tier (e.g., “Better Furnaces lll” costs 3 Value). 

Lastly, “Other” upgrades are upgrades that do not fit in the previous three categories and will have special rules listed for them.

These are special rules for playing against a special post-game boss; there might not be much challenge, but it is at least fun. The Mechanical Marquise uses pieces from the Riverfolk Expansion [C] and a shared supply of warriors.

The standard Mechanical Marquise v1.0 and set-up rules apply, with some small modifications:
-Since your armies both use the same pieces, the Mechanical Marquise’s Warriors will be placed upside down, balancing on the two “ears” of the figure. (This applies to the -Forest Patrol [C] Workshop upgrade, the Mechanical Marquise will prioritize Forest Patrol Warriors)
-You and the Mechanical Marquise share supplies. This means both you and the Mechanical Marquise have to compete for warriors. 
-The Mechanical Marquise does not use the Keep.
-In setup, you start only with the clearing with the Keep and the Adjacent clearings. The Mechanical Marquise starts the same in the opposite clearing.
-If it is difficult to tell which clearing is “opposite,” then set the Mechanical Marquise in any three adjacent clearings using standard ROOTbotics priority rules.


### Archipelago Support:
As of writing, I am writing a manual tool to get Archipelago support for this project. This will *only* be properly supported on the physical board game of ROOT, to respect the wishes of DireWolf Games and their rights to the digital version of ROOT. 

This can be accessed on the Manual Archipelago’s Discord chat or through my GitHub link here: https://github.com/Coolblubird/AROOTipelago/ . You may use and inspect the pre-published version to make your own, and a published copy of the code in the form of a .apworld is available under the releases.

The standard campaign rules apply, with the exceptions of the following:
-ALL purchases have been replaced with AP checks. 
-When you receive an item mid-game, you receive its effects immediately, and any effects that can’t trigger immediately are triggered at the beginning of the next round (Crafted Cards, and missed Headstart cards).
-You regain all lost lives when you receive an item, but do not have to restart upon losing all three, just pick up where you left off (alternatively, you may instead just make lives infinite, but the idea behind this rule is that you don’t need to grind through all of ROOT to enjoy the AP).
-Deathlink is exactly how you would think it works, like in similar Archipelagos, you instantly lose the round and must start again. Enable for only the brave.
